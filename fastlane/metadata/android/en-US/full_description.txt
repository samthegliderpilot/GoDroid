GoDroid lets you play the ancient game of Go on your Android phone or tablet - against the GnuGo computer engine, or against a friend sitting next to you.

Features
• Play against GnuGo or another person on the same device
• Japanese and Chinese rules
• 9×9, 13×13 and 19×19 boards
• Handicap stones, adjustable komi and adjustable computer strength
• Touch controls designed for placing stones accurately, including a mode that adds margins at the screen edges
• Undo, redo and pass
• Save and load games in the standard SGF format, in any folder you choose; add several games to one file
• Open .sgf files from other apps and share your games
• Optional delay before the computer moves, and an option to stop the computer from resigning
• Translated into many languages

GoDroid is a modernised fork of the original GOdroid by Andreas Grothe. No ads, no tracking, and it requests no permissions. Free software under the GPLv3.
